Decision layer

Revenue policy simulator

Ask high-risk bookings for a deposit or reconfirmation. Choose the risk threshold and your assumptions; the simulator replays the policy on all 2017 test bookings.

"Recovered" means the booking is kept, or the room is resold through a deposit, a reminder or an overbooking buffer. "Put off" means a guest who would have stayed cancels because of the deposit request.
